Strategic Partnership

Collaborative Delivery Model

Working alongside established accounting, assurance, and advisory firms (The "Partner Firm") to combine institutional reach with upstream sector depth.

1
Origination & Scoping
Both parties originate opportunities. Nexus leads or supports technical scoping and proposal development under agreed client routes.
2
Technical Delivery & Management
Nexus leads financial/commercial analysis and models; Partner Firm provides partner oversight and local supporting staff.
3
Joint Client Outcome
Integrated senior-led delivery under agreed branding, creating credible capacity without permanent recruitment overhead.

The Partner Firm May Contribute:

  • Client access, market presence, and business-development channels.
  • Institutional credentials, contracting, and engagement administration.
  • Partner oversight, supporting staff, and wider advisory capabilities.
  • Quality, risk management, and client-acceptance processes.

Nexus Energy Resources May Contribute:

  • Sector opportunity assessment, scoping, and bespoke methodology.
  • Technical proposal input, pricing support, and project planning.
  • Senior financial, commercial, and technical execution.
  • Client project management, reports, fiscal models, and board decks.
